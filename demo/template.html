Hear it. Sound it out. Spell it.

👨‍👩‍👧 For parents: this week's words

Type your child's spelling words (separated by spaces or commas). They join the word list for checking, and Practice can use just your list. Saved only on this device.

Prototype v1 — honest notes. A tiny model (2.5 KB) trained on the Dolch word list (314 words) with practice misspellings generated by code. On held-out generated spellings it finds the right word first about 87 times in 100, and it never changes a word that is already spelled right. It has not been tested on real children's writing yet. Everything runs on this device — nothing you type is sent anywhere.
